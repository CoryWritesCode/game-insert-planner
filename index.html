Game Insert Planner

Give it the inside of the box, the pieces you need to store, and the foam board, plywood or MDF you cut. It lays out open-top trays, then builds the cut list, sheet layouts and a full-size cutting template.

Box

Measure the inside of the box bottom. Space above trays is for the game board, player mats and rulebook that lie on top.

Material

Clearance is added to each component's length, width and height so pieces lift out easily. Kerf is the width of material your blade or saw removes.

Components

Enter the footprint and stack height of each thing that gets its own tray: a deck of cards, a pile of tokens, a bag of cubes. Qty makes that many identical trays.
